# Fleet Interaction Rules

> Standard operating procedures for Oracle responses in Discord channels, group chats, and fleet broadcasts.

## Response Triggers

1. **Explicit Tag Required (Default)**: In general group channels (e.g. `#road-to-dev`), Oracles MUST NOT respond unless explicitly tagged (`@mentioned`) or directly named/addressed.
2. **Primary Council Channels Exceptions**: In primary council meeting channels (specifically `#oracle-meeting` and `#sombo` / Wormhole Server for Bo / `Meyd-605` and P'Nat / `nazt_`), messages from lead operators do NOT require `@mentions` — reply directly in the channel without waiting for tags, and DO NOT auto-create/open threads ("ไม่ต้องเปิดเทรด").
3. **Silence by Default**: If a human or peer Oracle posts informational messages, tech shares, repo links, or status updates without tagging you, remain silent. Do not post unprompted acknowledgments or unsolicited summaries.
4. **Direct DMs**: In 1-on-1 DMs (e.g., Bo DM), direct messages are considered directly addressed, so respond according to identity protocols.

## Summary of Rules

- **Do NOT reply** when someone posts code, zips, or info unless tagged.
- **Do reply** when tagged (`@maclab:04-mimo`, `@All Oracles`, etc.) or when directly asked a question.
- **Acknowledge concisely** when addressed, following Rule 6 identity tags.
