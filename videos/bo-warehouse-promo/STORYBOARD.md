## Video direction

- **Palette**: `frame.md` is the color truth. Background uses `canvas` (#0c0a09, dark warm-black). Primary text uses `ink` (#f8f9fa). Highlights use `primary` (#1d4ed8, blue) and accents use `accent` (#d4af37, gold).
- **Motion**: Long-tail decel curves (`power3` default). Follow the **VO-paced reveal** model (reveal each piece on its spoken cue; never front-load). Hold frames use only `subtle-jitter` (`sine-wave-loop` low-amplitude) for aliveness; no breathing or bad slow pans/pushes.
- **Rhythm**: Frame 1 and Frame 3 are highly dynamic typography-driven sequences; Frame 2 is a held, steady visual showcase with a slow zoom/pan, functioning as a breather.
- **Negative List**: No off-brand AI gradients, no floating bokeh. No circular breathing or independent floating elements. No `repeat`/`yoyo` loops or `Math.random` particle systems. No mid-video exits.
- **Captions**: Bottom ~17% clear for caption-band keep-out.
